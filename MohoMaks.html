The companion’s accretion disc launches a broad bipolar wind. The illustrated lobes have denser, slower walls and faster, more tenuous inner flow.

About me

I’m Maksym Mohorian, an astrophysicist at Macquarie University in Sydney.

I study how gas and dust separate in discs around evolved binary stars. When gas returns to the stars, its chemical composition can leave a record of what happened in the disc.

I combine high-resolution stellar spectroscopy, radio interferometry and chemical modelling to connect those abundance patterns to the processes that shape the disc.
